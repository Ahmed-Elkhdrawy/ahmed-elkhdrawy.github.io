Animal Worlds Studio

Explore an animal's world in 60 seconds.

Animal Worlds Studio makes short, cinematic videos for the Animal Worlds Studio YouTube channel. In each one, an animal wearing a tiny action camera takes viewers into its hidden world.

About the "Animal Worlds Studio Uploader" app

The uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. After the owner reviews and approves a finished video, the tool:

  • uploads that video to the owner's own YouTube channel, with its title, description and settings;
  • checks the video's upload and processing status;
  • reads the channel's own video statistics, so the owner can see how videos perform.

It is not offered to the public, does not access anyone else's account, and does not publish anything without the owner's approval.
